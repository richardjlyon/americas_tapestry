Vicki Coco is an embroiderer on the Georgia tapestry panel. She and her husband live in Atlanta, Georgia, where needlecraft has been a constant in her life — Vicki enjoys sewing, quilting, and embroidery. She moved to Georgia from New York just in time to be part of the 1996 Olympic quilt project, helping make a quilt that was presented to the Olympic Organizing Committee of Monaco. Vicki has also made wedding dresses and christening gowns, sharing in her family's special memories. A few years ago she became a member of EGA's Dogwood Chapter, where she enjoys learning new techniques and skills. Her preferred work spans canvas, blackwork, crazy patch, and surface embroidery. It has been a privilege for Vicki to be part of America's Tapestry.
