# America's Tapestry: The Next Thread

*A pitch for a commercially funded series of state-based commemorative tapestry projects, 2027–2031*

## The Need

America is entering the richest run of anniversaries in a generation. The Revolution's 250th does not end on July 4, 2026 — Saratoga, Valley Forge, the French Alliance, and Yorktown carry it to 2031, with the Flag's 250th, aviation's triple anniversary, and railroading's bicentennial landing alongside. Communities want to mark these moments with something they made, not something they watched. Brands with heritage stories want authentic grassroots engagement, not another ad buy.

## The Gap

No organization in America can mobilize a multi-state volunteer craft network to deliver monumental commemorative art on schedule. Commemorations default to ceremonies, lectures, and fireworks — nothing tangible survives them. Corporate heritage marketing has budgets but no credible community vehicle. State anniversary commissions have mandates but no delivery capability.

## The Capability

We built one — from scratch. America's Tapestry recruited and organized 2,000 volunteers across 13 states, raised the funds, and delivered a complete multi-panel tapestry for the nation's 250th. The assets that remain are the business:

- A working state-director model and stitching-group network in 13 states
- A tested playbook: recruitment, panel design, production standards, quality control, deadlines met
- A digital platform, audience, and an e-commerce operation now launching
- A brand with proven earned-media reach in every participating state

## The Opportunity

Replicate the model as a series — one project every one to two years, each with a unifying theme and a defined set of states — and shift the funding base from donations to commerce, in three layers:

1. **Craft-industry title sponsor** (thread and fabric makers such as DMC or Anchor): negotiated once across the whole series. We deliver what no ad buy can — thousands of active stitchers who purchase supplies, plus press in every state.
2. **Theme anchor sponsor** per project: naming rights, per-panel state sponsorship, presence on the exhibition tour.
3. **Earned revenue**: companion stitch kits ($50–150, turning supporters into customers), licensed merchandise through our store, a book per project, exhibition hire fees, documentary rights.

A fourth stream inverts the model entirely: brands commission a tapestry outright for their own anniversary, and we are paid as producer.
