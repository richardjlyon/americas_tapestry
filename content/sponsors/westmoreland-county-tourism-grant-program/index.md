_America's Tapestry_ is proud to be the recipient of the _Westmoreland County Tourism Grant Program_. This opportunity offered through the tourism bureau GO Laurel Highlands is designed to "increase visitation, enhance the visitor experience, and increase overnight stays in Fayette, Somerset, and Westmoreland counties."

## About the Laurel Highlands

Located within 200 miles of the major metropolitan centers of Washington D.C., Philadelphia, Baltimore, Pittsburgh, and Cleveland, the Laurel Highlands offer memorable opportunities to explore the beauty of the outdoors.

Welcoming millions of visitors annually, this globally recognized destination showcases the arts, culture, and exceptional scenery that makes southwestern Pennsylvania so unique. Notable destinations include Frank Lloyd Wright's Fallingwater, five national parks, and the celebrated Seven Springs & Nemacolin resorts.

To learn more or to plan a visit, be sure to visit [GO Laurel Highlands](https://www.golaurelhighlands.com/).
