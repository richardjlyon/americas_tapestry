Founded in 2022, _Kindred Felt Co._ was created with the belief that every great project begins with quality materials and a spark of creativity. Based in Newport News, Virginia, the company specializes in premium wool blend felt and 100% wool felt, carefully selected to provide crafters, makers, educators, and small businesses with beautiful, durable materials they can trust.

Whether the project is handmade toys, appliqué, embroidery, seasonal décor, tapestries, quilts, quiet books, ornaments, accessories, or one-of-a-kind gifts, their felt is designed to inspire creativity while delivering exceptional color, texture, and lasting performance.

_Kindred Felt Co._ is passionate about offering a thoughtfully curated collection of vibrant, high-quality felt that helps bring every creative vision to life. The company is committed to providing exceptional materials, dependable service, and inspiration for makers at every stage of their creative journey.
